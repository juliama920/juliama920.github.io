SLC Punks presents:

A Night at Gino's

Come and enter Gino's, the club where the punking was born. This show will take you on a journey through punking's original influences and creators while exploring the Punks' own inspirations alongside other local club-style artists.

Put on your best club fit and come laugh, cry, and dance the night away at "A Night at Gino's!"

Who We Are

SLC Punks is a dance group made up of artists seeking to inspire, educate, and cultivate the culture of punking. Through events, classes, dance sessions, and performance opportunities, we intend to keep Punking alive and thriving in Utah
